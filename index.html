Do Huy Toan is an independent video creator based in Delaware, USA. His main channel, Do Huy Toan, shares glowing Christmas decoration ideas and short stories about vintage American holiday traditions. It has passed 33,000 subscribers and more than 24 million views (October 2026).

On JD, he makes A Day of JD, a wordless, ASMR-style animated series that follows a tiger cub through Taiwan. It is painted in a Monet-inspired style and animated frame by frame, and each episode has titles in five languages. Map a Day explains surprising geography facts in under a minute, using NASA Blue Marble satellite imagery.

Toan is open about his process. He uses AI image and video tools for the artwork, then builds the motion, sound and edit with his own code. He is interested in how a single creator can tell stories for audiences in many countries at once.

How it's made.

Open about the tools, careful about the craft.

Story first

Every episode starts with a script, a storyboard and a clear goal for the viewer. Facts are checked against two sources.

AI-assisted art

Paintings, characters and clips are generated with AI image and video tools, then selected and corrected by hand.

Motion & sound in code

Animation, camera moves, sound design and the final edit are built with the studio's own code.
