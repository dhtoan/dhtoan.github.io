Long · about 150 words

Do Huy Toan is an American entrepreneur based in Delaware, and the founder and CEO of an e-commerce and digital media company. He builds online stores, YouTube channels and interactive products, often as a one-person studio.

His main channel, Do Huy Toan, shares glowing Christmas decoration ideas and short stories about vintage American holiday traditions. It has passed 33,000 subscribers and more than 24 million views (October 2026).

On JD, he makes "A Day of JD", a wordless, ASMR-style animated series that follows a tiger cub through Taiwan, painted in a Monet-inspired style and titled in five languages. Map a Day explains surprising geography facts in under a minute, using NASA Blue Marble satellite imagery.

Toan is open about his process: AI tools create the artwork, while the motion, sound and edit are built with his own code.
