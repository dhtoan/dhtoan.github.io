Code-Animated Shorts from Generative AI Stills: A Practitioner Case Study on a DIY YouTube Channel

Do Huy Toan · 2 November 2026 · Working paper, version 1

A one-person pipeline that animates AI-generated still images with code, and how its YouTube Shorts performed on a DIY channel: 18 pipeline Shorts averaged 1.2K–2.5K views, against 26K–3.5M for the channel's Shorts built from AI-generated video clips.

DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23888335 · Zenodo record · PDF

AI use: Artwork in the videos described was generated with ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Canva; comparison Shorts used AI-generated video. The first draft of this paper was written with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) from the author's code, notes and YouTube Studio readings; the author checked every statement and is responsible for the content.
