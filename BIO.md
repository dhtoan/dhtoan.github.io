Do Huy Toan is an independent video creator based in Delaware, USA, who writes, directs and edits every video in his one-person studio.

His main channel, **Do Huy Toan**, shares glowing Christmas decoration ideas and short stories about vintage American holiday traditions. It has passed 33,000 subscribers and more than 24 million views (October 2026).

On **JD**, he makes "A Day of JD", a wordless, ASMR-style animated series that follows a tiger cub through Taiwan. It is painted in a Monet-inspired style and animated frame by frame, and each episode has titles in five languages.

**Map a Day** explains surprising geography facts in under a minute, using NASA Blue Marble satellite imagery.

Toan is open about his process. He uses AI image and video tools for the artwork, then builds the motion, sound and edit with his own code. He is interested in how a single creator can tell stories for audiences in many countries at once.
